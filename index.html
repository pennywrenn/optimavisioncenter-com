About the Practice

Care built around the patients other practices don't have time for.

Optima Vision Center is committed to excellence in serving your family's complete eye care needs — comprehensive exams, contact lens fittings, and a full optical department of high-fashion eyewear and designer frames. Dr. Firmin has built a practice that goes further than routine care: onsite, at-home, and in-office visits for patients other providers often turn away, including elderly patients, special-needs patients, and those with complex, trust-building needs. Nobody is taking care of their eyes except for us.

Meet Your Optometrist

Dr. Chelise Firmin, O.D.

Knowledgeable. Experienced. Trusted.

Licensed Optometrist

As a licensed optometrist, Dr. Firmin provides exams, diagnoses, and treatment for disorders that affect the eye and vision. Beyond training in eye diseases and disorders, she's trained in anatomy, biochemistry, and physiology — equipping her to recognize a wider range of health issues, including glaucoma, macular degeneration, diabetes, and hypertension.

Dr. Firmin is known for working with patients other providers pass on — special-needs adults and children, elderly patients, and those who need real time to build trust before a routine exam feels routine.
